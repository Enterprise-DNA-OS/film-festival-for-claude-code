# Recorded controls and source rules

Checked 18 November 2026. These checks find missing or conflicting records. They are not a classification service, legal opinion or guarantee of compliance. The responsible festival operator must inspect each source document and condition. A typed actor label does not prove identity.

| Check | Implemented behaviour | Source and limit |
|---|---|---|
| NZ exhibition evidence | Sign-off requires a classification route, label or exemption description, evidence reference and conditions for the edition jurisdiction. | [Classification Office: film festivals](https://www.classificationoffice.govt.nz/resources/items/film-festival-classification/). The office says films need a classification label before public exhibition. [Exemptions](https://www.classificationoffice.govt.nz/resources/items/exemptions-from-classification-and-labelling/) require a real applicable basis. The desk neither grants nor determines an exemption. |
| AU exhibition evidence | Records must match AU and carry the relevant decision or exemption evidence. | [Australian Classification: festivals and events](https://www.classification.gov.au/for-industry/festivals-and-events). Unclassified-event registration requirements and approved-institution exceptions need human verification. The base does not decide eligibility or register an event. |
| Exhibition rights | Checks recorded territory, licensed dates and maximum scheduled screening count. | [IPONZ: public use of copyright works](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/copyright/use-of-copyright-works/). Film use requires the appropriate permission. Dates and limits are the individual licence terms supplied by the operator, not universal legal thresholds. |
| Guest sharing purpose | Lists guests with overdue review dates or missing purpose or disclosure basis. | [NZ Privacy Principle 11](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/11/). Disclosures need an applicable basis; writing a sentence is not proof that one exists. |
| Guest retention review | Uses the operator's chosen review date and never deletes records automatically. | [NZ Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/). Retain personal information only as long as its lawful purpose requires. A review date is an internal control, not a statutory retention period. |
| Safe running order | Detects room overlaps including turnaround, unsupported projection formats and unchecked copies; sign-off needs a different operator label. | Festival operating policies in this project. These are not statutory requirements. Projectionists check playback, subtitles, keys and version outside this desk and record their evidence. |

Readiness uses a fingerprint of the underlying screening, film, edition, venue, licence and copy evidence. Changes make sign-off stale. New room conflicts or excess screening counts block it immediately. These controls do not verify that a cited document is authentic, interpret conditions, manage cinema encryption keys, check admission ages or prove the file screened matches the classified version.

For shared use, add authenticated identities, least-privilege database roles, protected report storage, backups and restore tests. Tables have row security enabled without public policies; the owner still bypasses it. This is not a multi-tenant access layer. Retention and lawful deletion procedures must cover source imports, notes, audit history, backups and generated documents. Do not store passports, card details or unnecessary personal data here.
